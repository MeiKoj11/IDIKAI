About us

This is merely an organization app and not there to give you "text-books" like teachings and exercises. This is meant for personal use and to create an organized space to aid your learning. You are in charge of your own curiosity of the language and immersion. This is not aimed for beginner learners but for those who aim to immerse themself in their target language as much as possible.

Journaling in your target language is an extremely important and overlooked practice which can accelerate your speaking ability. It's all about repetition and getting your brain used to the flow of the language so that eventually words and grammar simply become muscle memory.

Simple quick learning is also available when you can only spare a bit of time - these include flashcards, small translation tests and other short practice related to vocab and grammar points saved and created by you in order to keep your brain sharp in your TL.
